# GutterMax Launchpad

Build a modern, mobile-first, conversion-focused website for GutterMax Services, a [residential/commercial] gutter company serving Texas.

BRAND & TONE

- Professional, trustworthy, local, and friendly

- Colors: Green and White

- Logo: Gorilla

- Tagline idea: "Protect your home from the top down."

PAGES & SECTIONS

1. Home

   - Hero section with a strong headline, short subheadline, and a "Get a Free Estimate" button

   - Phone number in a sticky header with click-to-call on mobile

   - Services overview (cards with icons)

   - Why choose GutterMax: [licensed & insured, free estimates, fast scheduling, warranty, upfront pricing]

   - Before/after photo gallery

   - Customer reviews (3-5 testimonials with star ratings)

   - Service area section with a list of cities and a map

   - Final call-to-action banner

2. Services (a short section for each)

   - Gutter installation (seamless aluminum, copper, vinyl)

   - Gutter repair

   - Gutter cleaning

   - Gutter guards / leaf protection

   - Downspouts & drainage solutions

   - Fascia & soffit repair

   - [Add or remove as needed]

3. About Us: company story, team photo, years in business, licenses and certifications

4. Gallery: project photos with before/after sliders

5. Reviews: testimonials with links to Google reviews

6. FAQ:
